An AI voice agent is software that answers the phone, understands what the caller wants and does something about it. An AI receptionist is the most common kind: it greets callers, answers routine questions, books appointments and passes the rest to your team.

## How it differs from a phone menu

A traditional phone menu makes callers press buttons and hope their reason is on the list. A voice agent lets them speak normally, "I need to move my appointment to Thursday", and responds in a natural voice. It can be interrupted, asked to repeat itself, and will ask a follow-up question if something is unclear.

## What it handles well

- **Answering every call.** Including after hours, at lunch, and when all lines are busy.
- **Common questions.** Opening hours, location, prices, availability, accepted insurance.
- **Bookings.** Checking a real calendar, offering times, booking the slot and confirming by message.
- **Reminders.** Calling or messaging before an appointment to reduce no-shows.
- **Messages and routing.** Taking a clear message and sending it to the right person, or transferring the call live.
- **Notes.** Summarising each call and logging it in your CRM or booking system.

## Where a person is still needed

Complaints, anything clinical or legal, negotiation, and any call where the caller is distressed. A well-built agent recognises these and hands over quickly, with a summary, so the caller does not have to repeat themselves.

## What good practice looks like

- The agent says it is an AI assistant at the start of the call.
- Recording notices are in place where the law requires them.
- Outbound calls go only to people who have agreed to be contacted.
- It is tested on real past calls before it speaks to a customer.
- You choose the voice and approve the wording.

## Is your business ready for one?

It is usually worth a look if you can answer yes to two of these:

1. You miss calls, especially outside office hours.
2. A large share of calls are the same handful of requests.
3. Your bookings live in a calendar or system that software can read.
4. A missed call often means a lost customer.

## How to start

Start narrow. Let the agent take only after-hours and overflow calls, and only for bookings and common questions. Measure how many calls it resolves, how many it transfers and why. Widen the scope once you trust it.
