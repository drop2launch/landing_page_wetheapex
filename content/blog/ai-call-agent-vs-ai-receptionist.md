Three phrases come up whenever businesses look at automating their phones: IVR, AI receptionist and AI call agent. They overlap, but they are not the same thing.

## IVR: the phone menu

An IVR (interactive voice response) is the classic "press 1 for sales, press 2 for support" menu. It routes calls but does not understand them. Callers have to fit their question into your options, and anything unusual ends in a queue or a voicemail.

**Good for:** very high call volumes with a few clear categories.
**Weak at:** anything that needs understanding, booking or a real answer.

## AI receptionist: answers incoming calls

An AI receptionist answers your phone in a natural voice and lets the caller speak normally. It can:

- answer common questions about hours, prices, location and availability;
- book, move and cancel appointments in your real calendar;
- take a message and send it to the right person;
- transfer the call live when a person is needed.

**Good for:** clinics, salons, real estate agencies, restaurants, service businesses and any team that misses calls.

## AI call agent: also makes calls

An AI call agent does what an AI receptionist does, and also **calls out**:

- appointment reminders and confirmations, to reduce no-shows;
- call-backs to new leads within minutes of an enquiry;
- follow-ups after a quote or a visit.

Outbound calling has more rules than answering. Call only people who have agreed to be contacted, say at the start that the caller is an AI assistant, and check the regulations in each country you call.

**Good for:** businesses where speed of response wins customers, or where no-shows are expensive.

## How to choose

| If your main problem is… | Start with |
|---|---|
| Missed calls after hours or at busy times | AI receptionist |
| Leads going cold before anyone calls back | AI call agent |
| No-shows and late cancellations | AI call agent (reminders) |
| Very high volume, a few simple categories | IVR, or an AI receptionist in front of it |

Most businesses start with an AI receptionist for after-hours and overflow calls, measure how it performs, then add outbound calls.

## What to ask before you buy

- Does it connect to my real calendar or booking system?
- Which languages does it handle, and was it tested with real calls?
- Does it tell callers it is an AI?
- What happens when it cannot help?
- What does each minute of calling cost to run?
