In the UAE and India, customers would rather message a business on WhatsApp than call or email. That makes WhatsApp the best place to answer routine questions automatically, as long as it is done properly.

## What a WhatsApp assistant can do

- **Order and delivery updates.** "Where is my order?" answered instantly with a tracking link.
- **Bookings.** Appointments and reservations made, moved and confirmed.
- **Common questions.** Hours, prices, location, policies.
- **Lead capture.** New enquiries asked a few qualifying questions and passed to sales with the context.
- **Reminders and confirmations.** Appointments, payments, cash-on-delivery orders.
- **Handover.** Anything complex passed to a person, with the full conversation.

## Use the official API

There are two ways to automate WhatsApp: unofficial tools that drive the ordinary app, and the official WhatsApp Business API. Use the official one. Unofficial tools risk having your number banned, and they do not scale.

The API has rules worth knowing:

- Customers can message you first at any time, and you can reply freely within a 24-hour window.
- Messages you start yourself must use pre-approved templates.
- People must have opted in to receive messages from you.

## Old chatbots and AI assistants

Older bots made customers pick from numbered menus. An AI assistant understands a normal sentence, answers from your own content and data, and says when it does not know. For most businesses the best design is a mix: AI to understand the request, fixed rules for anything involving money or policy.

## Languages

In the UAE, plan for English and Arabic. In India, English first, with Hindi and regional languages where your customers use them. Test each language with real past messages, including the informal way people actually write.

## Five things to get right

1. **Start with one job.** Order status or bookings, not everything at once.
2. **Connect it to real data.** An assistant that cannot see the order or the calendar can only apologise.
3. **Make the handover easy.** A person should be one message away.
4. **Tell people it is automated.** It builds trust and avoids frustration.
5. **Review the conversations.** The questions it could not answer tell you what to improve next.

## What it costs to run

Meta charges per conversation or per message, depending on type and country, on top of your platform and AI usage. A good build estimates this before launch.
