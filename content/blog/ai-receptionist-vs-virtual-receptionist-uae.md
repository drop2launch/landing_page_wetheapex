If your business in the UAE misses calls, there are four common fixes: hire another receptionist, use a virtual receptionist or call answering service, outsource to a call centre, or set up an AI receptionist. Each one suits a different situation.

## Virtual receptionist and call answering services

A virtual receptionist is a person, usually in a shared call centre, who answers your calls from a script. They take messages, pass on basic information and sometimes book appointments.

- **Good for:** a personal touch on a small number of calls.
- **Limits:** they look after several businesses at once, so knowledge of yours is shallow. Bookings and messages are often emailed to you rather than entered into your systems, and coverage outside working hours costs more.

## Call centres

An outsourced call centre gives you a team of agents for larger volumes, such as a product launch or a customer service line.

- **Good for:** high, steady volumes of complex calls.
- **Limits:** training, minimum contracts and a monthly cost that grows with every agent.

## AI receptionist

An AI receptionist is voice AI that answers your number in a natural voice, in English and Arabic. It handles routine calls from start to finish and transfers the rest to your team.

- **Answers on the first ring, at any hour,** including Fridays, weekends, Ramadan hours and public holidays.
- **Handles several calls at once,** so a busy morning or a project launch does not mean a queue.
- **Works inside your systems:** it checks your calendar live, books the appointment, sends the confirmation on WhatsApp and logs a summary in your CRM.
- **Knows only what you approve:** your hours, services, prices and policies. When a call falls outside that, it hands over.

It is not right for every call. Complaints, sensitive conversations and complex sales calls should reach a person, and a good setup makes that transfer quick, with a summary so the caller does not repeat themselves.

## Which one should you choose?

| Your situation | Usually the best fit |
| --- | --- |
| Missed calls after hours and at busy times | AI receptionist |
| Most calls are bookings, prices, hours and directions | AI receptionist |
| A few high-value calls a day that need a personal touch | Your own team, with AI as overflow |
| Large volumes of complex support calls | Call centre, with AI for routine calls |
| A launch or campaign with a sudden spike in calls | AI receptionist, with sales taking the booked call-backs |

Most businesses end up with a mix: AI for the routine calls, people for the ones that need judgement.

## What to check before you buy

1. **Your number stays the same.** Calls should forward from your existing business line, either always or only when nobody picks up.
2. **English and Arabic,** tested with real sample calls in both languages, with your own names, areas and terms.
3. **Disclosure and recording.** Callers should be told they are speaking with an AI assistant. If calls are recorded, callers must be told and the recordings handled in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law.
4. **Real integration.** Bookings should go straight into your calendar or booking system, not into an email someone has to retype.
5. **A clear handover rule.** You decide which calls go to a person, and the transfer works during busy hours too.
6. **Your data stays yours,** and is not used to train AI models.

## Where to start

Begin with the calls you already know you miss: after hours, lunchtimes and peak periods. Put the AI receptionist on those first, listen to the calls for a week or two, then decide how much more it should handle.
