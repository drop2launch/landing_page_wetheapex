## What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Local storage does the same job but stays on your device and is not sent with every request. The law treats both the same way: anything that is not strictly necessary needs your consent first.

## Essential storage

These are needed for features you use, so they do not need consent. They stay on your device and are never sent to us.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Lasts |

| `apex-currency` | Local storage | Remembers the currency you chose in the savings estimate | Until you clear it |
