## Which laws this policy follows

We are based in the UAE and work with businesses in the UAE, the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, India, Saudi Arabia, Australia and Singapore. This policy is written to meet the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, "PDPL"), and to give you the information and rights required by:

- the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the UK GDPR;
- US state privacy laws, including California's CCPA as amended by the CPRA;
- Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25;
- India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023;
- Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law;
- Australia's Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles;
- Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

Some of these laws apply only to businesses above a certain size. We offer the rights described below to everyone, wherever they live.

## How we protect it

- Connections to this site are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Access to enquiries is limited to the people who need it, using accounts with two-factor authentication.
- API keys and passwords are stored on the server, never in the browser or in code.
- We use reputable providers that maintain recognised security programmes.

No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the relevant regulator within the time the law requires. See our [Security & data handling](/security) page for how we protect client data on projects.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- **Tell you** what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and give you a copy (**access**);
- **Correct** or complete data that is wrong or out of date;
- **Delete** your data;
- **Restrict** how we use it, or **object** to us using it, including for direct marketing;
- Send your data to you or another organisation in a portable format (**portability**);
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting what we did before.

Some rights have legal limits. For example, we may need to keep records the law requires. If we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why.

**How to ask:** email {{email}}. We will confirm receipt, may ask you to confirm your identity, and will reply within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. You may use an authorised agent; we may ask them for proof that you have authorised them. We will never treat you differently for using your rights.

### Additional information by country

- **United Arab Emirates:** you also have the right to object to decisions based solely on automated processing. Complaints can be made to the UAE Data Office.
- **European Union and United Kingdom:** you can complain to your local data protection authority, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced them against your rights; you can ask us for details.
- **United States:** in the past 12 months we have collected identifiers (such as name, email and IP address), professional information (such as company and role) and, if you accept analytics, internet activity. We collected them for the purposes listed above. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. Because analytics stay off unless you opt in, we honour browser privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control by default. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if your appeal is refused, you can contact your state Attorney General.
- **Canada:** you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information. The person responsible for protecting personal information is named below.
- **India:** you have the right to grievance redressal and to nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to. Please contact our grievance contact below first; if you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Saudi Arabia:** you can complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).
- **Australia:** if you are not satisfied with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). You can deal with us anonymously or under a pseudonym where that is practical, for example when asking a general question in the chat.
- **Singapore:** you can contact our data protection officer below, and the Personal Data Protection Commission.
